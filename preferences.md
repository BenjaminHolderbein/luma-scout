# Ben's event preferences

Ben is an MSDS grad student at USF, based in San Francisco, currently with
fully open availability. He's optimizing for high-value, in-person SF events.

## Priority tiers (STRICT, highest first)

An event maps to the **highest** tier it matches. All of a higher tier ranks
above all of a lower tier.

1. **🛠️ Hackathon** — hackathons, buildathons, build weekends, hack nights, jams.
   Building/competing/shipping, often with prizes.
2. **🍕 Free food/drink** — food and/or drink is *provided free*: dinner served,
   open bar, bites/apps provided, catered lunch, happy hour with complimentary
   drinks. (See "Food rubric" — must be free/provided, not a cash bar.)
3. **🤝 Networking** — mixers, socials, meetups, community hangs, "meet fellow
   founders/builders", office hours framed around connecting.
4. **🎓 Seminar** — talks, panels, fireside chats, workshops, lectures, demos, AMAs.

An event matching **none** of these four → **drop it** (don't notify).

## Hard exclude

- **Defense / military / weapons** events → always drop. Dual-use / grey-area
  tech (e.g. general robotics, security research, gov-adjacent civic tech) is OK.

## Ranking signals within a tier (soft nudge, in priority order)

1. **Free admission beats paid.** Within a tier, free-to-attend ranks above paid.
   Paid events are still included, just demoted.
2. **Relevance to Ben's field** — AI/ML, LLMs, agents, evals, data science,
   analytics, MLOps, startups/founders, applied engineering. Nudge these up.
   (Not a hard filter — a great free-food/networking event in an unrelated field
   still counts; relevance just breaks ties.)
3. **Signal of a good room** — reasonable crowd size, notable hosts/companies/
   speakers, not a giant impersonal mixer with no substance.
4. **RSVP urgency** — near-capacity / filling fast / waitlist-forming events get a
   nudge so Ben can act in time.

## Food rubric (for the #2 tier — this is the fuzziest call)

- COUNTS as free food/drink: "dinner provided", "open bar", "drinks & bites",
  "food and drinks served", "catered", "happy hour" (drinks implied free),
  "pizza/snacks provided", "breakfast/lunch included".
- Does NOT count: "cash bar", "drinks available for purchase", "food trucks
  on site" (pay), "no-host bar", or events that simply happen at a bar/restaurant
  with no indication anything is provided.
- If genuinely ambiguous, do NOT promote to the food tier — rank it by whatever
  other tier it clearly matches.

## Tone for summaries

Terse and useful. Lead with the concrete hook Ben cares about (the free dinner,
the prize, who's in the room), then one line on what it is. No hype, no fluff.
